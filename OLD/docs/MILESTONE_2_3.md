# Test checklist

1. Move around the map and confirm objective direction/distance updates.
2. Stand near an enemy grenade and confirm the warning appears.
3. Damage an enemy and confirm damage plus remaining health appears.
4. Damage a target, let a teammate finish it, and confirm ASSIST.
5. Earn three eliminations without dying and confirm KILL STREAK.
6. Jump from elevated geometry and confirm fall damage.
7. Start the VPS with `--bot-skill 0.5` and compare accuracy.
8. Restart with `--bot-skill 1.5` and confirm bots are more accurate.
