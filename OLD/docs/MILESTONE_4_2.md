# Test checklist

1. Open the project and allow Godot to import all PNG and GLB assets.
2. Explore the far-west village and confirm imported townhouse models appear.
3. Inspect cobblestone, brick, plaster, and rubble materials at close range.
4. Confirm normal maps react to directional and street lighting.
5. Switch between rifle and pistol and inspect imported arms and hands.
6. Confirm recoil, aiming, sprint lowering, and muzzle flash still work.
7. Walk into visual buildings and confirm collision is present.
8. Start the Linux headless server and confirm it skips visual asset loading.
9. Confirm existing objectives, bots, sectors, and TAB results remain.
10. Confirm protocol 341 and the explicit connection-message `+`.
