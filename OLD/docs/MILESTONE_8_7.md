# Test checklist

1. View Allied and Axis soldiers from the front, side, and rear.
2. Confirm heads and hands no longer receive the uniform fabric texture.
3. Confirm rifles, helmets, and buckles respond as metal under direct light.
4. Inspect shoulder straps, breast pockets, buttons, pouches, and belt hardware.
5. Confirm Allied and Axis helmet silhouettes are visibly different.
6. Inspect the rounded boots and backpack from the side.
7. Inspect rifle handguards, bolts, trigger guards, sights, and slings.
8. Confirm walking, running, crouching, downed, and aiming animation still works.
9. Change class and confirm medic, engineer, radio, and scope gear remains present.
10. Raise Music volume and confirm battlefield ambience is clearly audible.
11. Wait through the ambience ending and confirm it loops without stopping.
12. Install one licensed external soldier and confirm it still replaces fallback art.
13. Confirm v8.7.0 appears in the connection panel and server log.
14. Confirm protocol 341 remains compatible with v8.6 clients.
