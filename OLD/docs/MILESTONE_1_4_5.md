# Spawn validation test

1. Start the VPS with eight bots.
2. Check every `Spawned ... at ...` log line.
3. Join Attackers and deploy repeatedly.
4. Join Defenders and deploy repeatedly.
5. Confirm no player appears inside cover, above the river, or outside the ground.
6. Use M to switch teams several times.
7. Kill/respawn several actors and confirm alternates are selected when occupied.
