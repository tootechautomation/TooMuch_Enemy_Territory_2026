# Milestone 8.25 — Class-Specific First-Person Weapon Rigs

Version 8.25 replaces the shared stacked-placeholder long gun with coherent class-specific fallback silhouettes.

## Support LMG

The Soldier fallback now presents one drum-fed light machine gun with a jacketed barrel, rear sight, carry handle, and compact folded bipod. The generic vertical magazine and bright floor plate are not created for this profile.

## Other class profiles

- Medic: compact SMG stick magazine, base plate, and barrel shroud.
- Engineer: shorter carbine magazine and wood fore-end.
- Field Ops: restrained service-rifle box magazine and grenade sight.
- Scout: compact rifle magazine paired with the existing scope assembly.

## Grip and stock correction

The firing hand converges on the rear grip and the support hand converges on the forward handguard. A layered stock body, comb, and butt plate replace the former oversized wood box. The complete long-gun viewmodel is positioned farther from the camera to preserve the center sight picture.

## Compatibility

Imported first-person scenes retain priority. Reload-part naming remains compatible with the existing slide, bolt, charging-handle, magazine, drum, and support-arm animation system. Gameplay, balance, networking, and protocol 341 are unchanged.
