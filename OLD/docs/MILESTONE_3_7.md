# Test checklist

1. Capture the western supply depot.
2. Confirm four tickets are immediately restored.
3. Hold it for twelve seconds and confirm another ticket is restored.
4. Contest it with an enemy and confirm the contested state.
5. Deploy as Field Ops and press V.
6. Die and confirm the next wave uses the rally point.
7. Move an enemy within eight meters and confirm rally respawns stop.
8. Call artillery near bots and confirm they move away.
9. Confirm mission banners appear for depot captures and rally deployment.
10. Confirm connection failure text parses with the explicit `+`.
