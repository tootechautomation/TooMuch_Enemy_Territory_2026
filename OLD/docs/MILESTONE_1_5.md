# Test checklist

1. Validate the project.
2. Deploy as Attackers.
3. Confirm the HUD shows approximately five seconds of protection.
4. Let a bot shoot during protection; health should remain unchanged.
5. Wait for protection to expire and confirm damage works.
6. Respawn, fire immediately, and confirm protection ends.
7. Respawn, throw a grenade, and confirm protection ends.
8. Confirm blue and red spawn-zone floor markers are visible.
