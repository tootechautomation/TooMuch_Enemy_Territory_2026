# Test checklist

1. Validate the project.
2. Connect a Windows client.
3. Confirm the deployment menu appears.
4. Choose Attackers and Engineer, then deploy.
5. Confirm HUD team/class update.
6. Press M and switch to Defenders and Medic.
7. Confirm the player respawns at the opposite side.
8. Confirm WASD does not move while the menu is open.
