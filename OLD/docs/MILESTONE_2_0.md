# Test checklist

1. Start with eight bots.
2. Watch bots leave spawn and move toward objectives.
3. Confirm opposing bots engage one another.
4. Kill a bot and confirm it disappears immediately.
5. Confirm the bot returns on the next respawn wave.
6. Confirm dead bots do not block movement or bullets.
7. Observe engineers advancing toward objectives.
8. Observe medics attempting revives.
9. Watch bots jump over low obstacles or recover when stuck.
10. Confirm bots reload and use Q abilities.
