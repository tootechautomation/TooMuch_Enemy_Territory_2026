# Test checklist

1. Confirm main.gd parses without a get_world_3d error.
2. Start the VPS and confirm build v5.8.0 protocol 341.
3. Fire at a bot and watch it select lateral cover.
4. Confirm bots do not rapidly switch cover every frame.
5. Watch medics, engineers, Field Ops, scouts, and soldiers retain class roles.
6. Confirm objective-stage anchors still change after bridge completion.
7. Confirm bot respawn clears old threat and cover state.
8. Verify ground collision, fall recovery, and wall collision remain.
9. Verify surface-aware footsteps remain.
10. Confirm normal multiplayer combat and objectives.
