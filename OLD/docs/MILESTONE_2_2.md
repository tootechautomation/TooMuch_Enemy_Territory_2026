# Test checklist

1. Validate the project.
2. Shoot a bot high on the capsule and confirm HEADSHOT appears.
3. Confirm tracers and impact flashes appear for human and bot shots.
4. Watch bots occasionally throw grenades at medium range.
5. Confirm the objective progress bar changes during construction.
6. Down a human teammate and confirm the body and REVIVE marker remain visible.
7. Confirm dead bots still disappear.
8. Press Tab and review the expanded scoreboard.
9. Finish a round and confirm the final results screen appears.
10. Confirm the next round starts automatically and hides the results.
11. Explore the new side cover, raised platforms, and center cover.
