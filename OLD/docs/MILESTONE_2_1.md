# Test checklist

1. Start the match and confirm BUILD THE BRIDGE appears.
2. Deploy as Engineer and approach the yellow zone.
3. Hold E and confirm construction progress changes.
4. Complete the bridge and confirm the bunker marker replaces it.
5. Arm dynamite and confirm the red model, pulse, and fuse.
6. Join Defenders as Engineer and confirm the defuse prompt.
7. Defuse and confirm the charge model disappears.
8. Let a charge explode and confirm the objective/match updates.
