# Test checklist

1. Confirm the desktop project starts with Forward+ shown in the editor title.
2. Inspect brick, plaster, concrete, wood, metal, cobblestone, and slate up close.
3. Confirm surface relief reacts to the sun instead of appearing flat-painted.
4. Confirm rough metal and wet/dark ground reflect light differently.
5. Inspect building corners and rubble for stronger contact shadows.
6. Confirm distant structures blend into atmospheric fog without disappearing.
7. Inspect sandbag walls and confirm individual bags are rounded, not boxes.
8. Inspect rubble fields and confirm stones have irregular silhouettes.
9. Check windows and damp surfaces for restrained screen-space reflections.
10. Confirm edges remain stable with 4x MSAA and screen-space antialiasing.
11. Run the mobile renderer once and confirm the GL fallback still starts.
12. Confirm headless server startup remains unaffected by the renderer change.
13. Confirm v8.6.0 appears in the connection panel and server log.
14. Confirm protocol 341 remains compatible with v8.5 clients.
