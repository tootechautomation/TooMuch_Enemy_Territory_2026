# Test checklist

1. Test 1280x720, 1600x900, and 1920x1080.
2. Resize the game window while running.
3. Confirm the compass remains fully inside the upper-right corner.
4. Confirm the kill feed does not overlap the compass.
5. Walk close to spawn beams and confirm they disappear.
6. Inspect objective, command-post, sector, and cover labels.
7. Confirm world labels no longer dominate the screen.
8. Hold TAB and confirm the scoreboard scales correctly.
9. Test fullscreen and windowed modes.
10. Confirm protocol 341 and all gameplay systems.
