# Test checklist

1. Fire across a long sightline and confirm the tracer is short.
2. Confirm wall impacts no longer create large glowing spheres.
3. Fire indoors and inspect the brief muzzle lighting.
4. Confirm the crosshair remains readable but less dominant.
5. Find the new wooden barricades.
6. Inspect burning barrels and their nearby light.
7. Observe distant smoke columns.
8. Confirm smoke and fire do not block primary spawn views.
9. Confirm the native compass and v5.0 objective arrow still work.
10. Confirm protocol 341 and all gameplay systems.
