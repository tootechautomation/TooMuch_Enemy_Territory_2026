# Test checklist

1. Deploy as Soldier and press Q; reserve ammo and grenade count should increase.
2. Deploy as Medic near an injured teammate and press Q; teammate health should increase.
3. Deploy as Engineer near the objective and press Q; objective progress should advance.
4. Deploy as Field Ops near teammates and press Q; their reserve ammunition should increase.
5. Deploy as Scout near enemies and press Q; enemy players should display SPOTTED.
6. Confirm every class HUD changes from READY to a 12-second countdown.
7. Confirm Q cannot activate again until the countdown reaches READY.
