# Test checklist

1. Build the bridge and confirm the command post unlocks.
2. Enter the command-post radius as one team and watch capture progress.
3. Add more teammates and confirm capture accelerates.
4. Add an enemy and confirm CONTESTED appears.
5. Complete capture and confirm the team uses forward spawns.
6. Confirm capture restores five tickets.
7. Eliminate players and confirm their team loses tickets.
8. Reduce a team to zero tickets and confirm dead players stop respawning.
9. Confirm the team loses once no living players remain.
10. Let the timer expire during an armed charge and confirm overtime.
11. Clear the objective and confirm overtime ends.
12. Observe lighting shifting toward dusk during the round.
