# Milestone 0.8 test checklist

1. Validate the project in Godot.
2. Start a server with `--bots 8`.
3. Confirm the terminal prints that eight bots spawned.
4. Allow a bot to empty its magazine and confirm it reloads.
5. Die and confirm the camera follows a living teammate.
6. Press F while dead to cycle teammate views.
7. Finish a round and confirm the next round begins after ten seconds.
8. Hold E at objectives instead of repeatedly tapping it.
