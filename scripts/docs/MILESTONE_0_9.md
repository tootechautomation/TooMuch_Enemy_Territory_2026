# Milestone 0.9 test checklist

1. Validate the project.
2. Connect a Windows client.
3. Fire the rifle and confirm rifle ammunition decreases.
4. Press X and confirm the pistol model and pistol ammunition appear.
5. Fire the pistol, switch back, and confirm rifle ammunition was preserved.
6. Damage an enemy and confirm the brief center-screen hit marker.
7. Empty either weapon, reload, switch away, and switch back.
8. Confirm bots still fire and reload.
