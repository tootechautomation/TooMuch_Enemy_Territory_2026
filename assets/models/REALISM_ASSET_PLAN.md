# Realism Asset Plan

The procedural geometry is a fallback and cannot visually match a cinematic
concept render. The next visual milestone must replace geometry, not merely
recolor it.

Required licensed assets:

## Characters
- Allied infantry rigged humanoid GLB
- Axis infantry rigged humanoid GLB
- 2K or 4K PBR uniform and skin materials
- Animations: idle, walk, run, crouch, aim, fire, reload, grenade, hit, downed,
  revive, and death

## Environment
- Modular European/North African WWII walls and buildings
- Door, window, roof, balcony, stair, arch, and rubble modules
- Cobblestone, damaged plaster, brick, concrete, wood, and metal PBR sets
- Sandbags, crates, barrels, carts, street lamps, vehicles, foliage, and debris

## Technical requirements
- Original, CC0, public-domain, or commercially licensed for game distribution
- GLB/GLTF preferred
- Real-world meter scale
- Collision-friendly low-poly proxy meshes
- LODs where available
- No extracted Wolfenstein: Enemy Territory assets
