# Test checklist

1. Finish a round and confirm only one victory/results overlay appears.
2. Hold TAB during the results screen; no second scoreboard should open.
3. Confirm the bottom combat HUD and compass disappear during results.
4. Confirm the kill feed hides during results.
5. Watch the restart countdown update cleanly.
6. Begin a new round and confirm the results overlay disappears.
7. Approach teammates and inspect compact name/class labels.
8. Move away and confirm labels disappear quickly.
9. Check that spotted markers are no longer visible through every wall.
10. Confirm protocol 341 and all gameplay systems remain active.
