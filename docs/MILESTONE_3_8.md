# Test checklist

1. Earn XP and confirm the rank-progress HUD changes.
2. Reach a new rank and respawn.
3. Confirm maximum health and reserve ammunition increase.
4. Damage an enemy and let a teammate finish them.
5. Confirm an assist appears on the scoreboard.
6. Perform objective actions and confirm objective score increases.
7. Finish a round and inspect all round awards.
8. Start the next round and confirm K/D/A, objective score, and round XP reset.
9. Confirm total XP and rank remain after the round reset.
10. Confirm the connection failure message still contains the explicit `+`.
