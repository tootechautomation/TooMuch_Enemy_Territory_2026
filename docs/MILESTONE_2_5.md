# Test checklist

1. Sprint until stamina drains and confirm sprint stops.
2. Stop sprinting and confirm stamina regenerates.
3. Take enemy damage and confirm SUPPRESSED plus red tint.
4. Fire while suppressed and compare accuracy.
5. Use Medic Q and confirm a green health pack appears.
6. Use Field Ops Q and confirm a yellow ammo pack appears.
7. Walk over a matching team pack and confirm it is consumed.
8. Fire and confirm a brass shell ejects.
9. Middle-click a location and confirm teammates see the marker.
10. Confirm enemies do not see the team ping.
