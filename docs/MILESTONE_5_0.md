# Test checklist

1. Confirm the entire circular compass is visible at 1280x720.
2. Resize the window and confirm the compass remains top-right.
3. Rotate the player and inspect rotating compass ticks.
4. Confirm the objective diamond moves around the compass edge.
5. Confirm teammate and enemy markers remain inside the circle.
6. Test the left/right/forward objective arrow.
7. Inspect fences, poles, craters, and direction signs.
8. Confirm the weapon blocks less of the center view.
9. Confirm end-of-round results and TAB presentation remain clean.
10. Confirm protocol 341 and all gameplay systems.
