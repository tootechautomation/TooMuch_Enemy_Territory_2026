# Milestone 0.6 testing checklist

1. Start one headless server.
2. Connect two clients.
3. Select Engineer with key 3.
4. As an attacker, press E ten times at the yellow bridge marker.
5. Cross the constructed bridge.
6. Press E at the bunker to arm the charge.
7. As a defending Engineer, press E five times at the bunker to defuse.
8. Rearm and allow the eight-second fuse to expire.
9. Confirm XP and rank appear in the HUD and Tab scoreboard.

Known limitation: interactions are discrete key presses rather than hold-to-progress.
