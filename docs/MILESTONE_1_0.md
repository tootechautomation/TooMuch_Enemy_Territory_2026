# Milestone 1.0 test checklist

1. Validate the project in Godot.
2. Start a server with `--bots 8`.
3. Connect a Windows client.
4. Press G and confirm the grenade travels forward.
5. Confirm the grenade bounces and detonates after about three seconds.
6. Confirm nearby enemies take more damage than distant enemies.
7. Confirm teammates do not take grenade damage.
8. Confirm the HUD starts with two grenades and decrements after throwing.
9. Die and respawn; confirm grenade inventory returns to two.
10. End a round while a grenade exists; confirm the new round starts cleanly.
