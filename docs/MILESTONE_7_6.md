# Test checklist

1. Join with a persistent profile.
2. Press F7 and inspect the career panel.
3. Complete a round and verify match history.
4. Restart the server and reconnect.
5. Confirm career totals persist.
6. Rename the player and verify the same record loads.
7. Confirm bots are excluded.
8. Confirm F8, TAB and K still work.
9. Confirm no parser errors.
10. Confirm v7.6.0 protocol 341.
