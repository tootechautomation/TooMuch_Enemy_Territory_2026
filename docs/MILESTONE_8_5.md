# Test checklist

1. Fire into metal barrels or rails and listen for a bright positional ring.
2. Fire into crates or doors and listen for a short wooden thump.
3. Compare brick walls with concrete or stone structures.
4. Fire into roads and ground and confirm the impact sounds muted and low.
5. Shoot another player and confirm the impact sound remains restrained.
6. Move around an impact point and confirm left/right spatial positioning.
7. Fire a long automatic burst and confirm impact audio remains stable.
8. Confirm rapid impacts vary slightly in pitch rather than repeating exactly.
9. Confirm master and effects volume settings still control combat audio.
10. Confirm headless servers create no audio players.
11. Confirm v8.5.0 appears in the connection panel and server log.
12. Confirm protocol 341 remains compatible with v8.4 clients.
