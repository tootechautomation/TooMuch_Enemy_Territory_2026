# Test checklist

1. Fire into red brick and confirm the bullet hole remains after debris fades.
2. Wait roughly 12 seconds and confirm the decal fades cleanly.
3. Fire into walls from several angles and confirm debris exits the surface.
4. Fire into metal and confirm brief orange ricochet streaks appear.
5. Fire repeatedly and confirm the oldest decals are removed after the cap.
6. Confirm brick, concrete, wood, ground, and metal colors remain distinct.
7. Trigger grenade explosions and confirm scorch and debris effects remain.
8. Confirm headless server startup creates no visual effect nodes.
9. Connect an 8.3.x client and confirm protocol 341 compatibility.
10. Confirm v8.4.0 is shown in the connection panel and server log.
