# Test checklist

1. Observe bots walking and sprinting; confirm arms and legs animate.
2. Watch a crouched player and confirm the model lowers.
3. Confirm backpacks and helmets move subtly during locomotion.
4. Inspect the circular compass in the upper-right.
5. Confirm teammate, enemy, grenade, and objective radar markers remain.
6. Rotate the player and verify heading/cardinal direction updates.
7. Move toward the objective and verify distance decreases.
8. Confirm the kill feed is readable and no longer overlaps the radar.
9. Hold TAB and inspect the improved typography.
10. Confirm protocol 341 and the explicit connection-message `+`.
