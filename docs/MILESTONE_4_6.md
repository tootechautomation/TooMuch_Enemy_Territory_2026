# Test checklist

1. Spawn and verify no helmet, head, torso, legs, or backpack are visible.
2. Confirm only the weapon, hands, sleeves, and arms appear in first person.
3. Observe another player or bot and confirm their full body remains visible.
4. Respawn and switch classes; confirm the local body stays hidden.
5. Inspect the new bottom-left health/rank panel.
6. Inspect the new bottom-right weapon/ammunition panel.
7. Confirm the timer, tickets, and objective appear at the top center.
8. Hold TAB and inspect separate Attackers and Defenders sections.
9. Confirm the normal HUD hides while TAB, the spawn menu, or tactical map is open.
10. Confirm protocol 341 and all existing gameplay systems still operate.
